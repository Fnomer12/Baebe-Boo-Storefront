# Till printing — one page for the shop

Print this and keep it by the till.

## First time on a new till (do once)

1. Make sure the label printer is plugged into the till and switched on.
2. On the till, open the Baebe Boo counter page and press **Download till setup**.
3. Open the downloaded file and double-click **SETUP-TILL-PRINTER**. Click Yes.
4. Wait until the window says **DONE** (a few minutes). If it asks anything, say yes.
5. Back on the Baebe Boo page, press **Retry**. Pick the printer, print the test,
   and press **I see it** only if paper actually came out.

## Every day

Nothing. Printing just works once the setup above is done.

## If receipts or labels stop printing

1. Is the printer switched on? Is paper loaded? Is the USB cable in?
2. On the till, open the Baebe Boo page and press **Retry** on the printer box.
3. Still stuck? Double-click **SETUP-TILL-PRINTER** again and wait for DONE.
4. Still stuck? Take a photo of any error on screen and send it to support.

## Selling by scanning (counter)

1. On the Sell screen, press **Scan with this till's camera**.
2. Allow the camera the first time it asks.
3. Point at the sticker QR or barcode. It beeps and adds the item — keep scanning the basket.
4. Take payment as normal. If a scan will not add, type the code by hand in the scan box.

Do not unplug the printer while it is printing. Do not install anything else for printing.
